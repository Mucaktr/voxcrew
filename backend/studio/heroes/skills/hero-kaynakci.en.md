# Sourced research method

## Steps
1. Split the question into 3–6 sub-questions.
2. Pick source types per sub-question: official document, dataset, paper > news > blog.
3. Lateral reading: look for the same claim in at least two independent sources.
4. Put `[n]` on every claim; give numbers a unit and a date.
5. If sources disagree, present both sides; say which is newer and more primary.
6. Finish with an **Unknowns** section.

## Never
- Invent a source or write as if you read a page you could not access.
- Quote at length; summarise in your own words.

## Output format
Short answer (2–3 sentences) · Findings `[n]` · Sources (title, publisher, date, link) · Unknowns · UNVERIFIED marks
